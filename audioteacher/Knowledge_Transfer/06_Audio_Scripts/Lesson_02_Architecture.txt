Welcome back student. Today we are going to dive deep into the Medallion Data Architecture.
The Medallion Architecture organizes data into three progressive refinement layers: Bronze, Silver, and Gold.
The Bronze layer acts as raw landing. It ingests transactional delta records directly from source tables and stores raw JSON payloads with extraction timestamps and batch IDs.
The Silver layer cleanses and standardizes this data. It decodes raw JSON, casts data types, handles missing null values, masks sensitive customer PII, and isolates corrupt records into quarantine tables.
The Gold layer implements Kimball Dimensional Modeling. It builds 14 Slowly Changing Dimensions with effective dates and current flags, 6 Fact tables, and 2 analytical business marts.
Let's pause and understand this with an example. Bronze is like raw wheat harvested from a farm. Silver is refined flour with impurities filtered out. Gold is a freshly baked loaf of bread ready for consumers.
