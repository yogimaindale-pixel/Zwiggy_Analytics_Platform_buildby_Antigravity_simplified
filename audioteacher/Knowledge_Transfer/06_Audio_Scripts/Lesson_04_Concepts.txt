Welcome back student. Today we are going to master core data engineering concepts, including Data Quality firewalls and Slowly Changing Dimensions.
Data Quality is the cornerstone of trustworthy analytics. Our pipeline evaluates seven mandatory quality rules, measuring completeness, uniqueness, referential integrity, and range bounds.
If an order contains a negative total amount, the Data Quality firewall catches it and routes the record to quarantine.
Another key concept is Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2. When a customer updates their delivery address, we do not overwrite the old address. Instead, we close the historical record with an effective end date and insert a new active record with a current flag set to one.
Let's pause and understand this with an example. Think of SCD Type 2 like keeping a photo album of your customer over time rather than overwriting their single profile photo.
Now let's see where this appears in the repository. Data Quality rule evaluation is coded in src zwiggy dwh dq dot py, while SCD Type 2 transformations are implemented in src zwiggy dwh gold dot py.
